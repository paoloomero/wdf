How rivers shape valleys

By A. Author · May 2, 2026

Rivers carve valleys through erosion and sediment transport. Over thousands of years, a stream deepens its bed and widens its banks.

Three stages

  1. youthful, with steep gradients;
  2. mature, with meanders;
  3. old age, with floodplains.

Fun fact: the Grand Canyon exposes nearly two billion years of rock.
