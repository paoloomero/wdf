Energy consumption report for municipal buildings 2023-2025

Summary

Over the 2023-2025 period the electricity consumption of municipal buildings fell from 182400 kWh to 158300 kWh (about 13 per cent), while spending rose from 43776 euros to 52239 euros due to the increase in unit prices. Completed efficiency measures delivered verified savings; for the most recent measure the verification is scheduled for 2026.

Methodological note

Values are expressed in canonical units (whole kWh, euros with a dot decimal separator) as the format requires: locale-specific formatting of amounts is left to future extensions. Data source: internal monitoring system, readings taken on 31 December of each year.
